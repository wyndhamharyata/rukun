The test app: Rukun for the desktop, built four times

Version 3, 9 November 2026. Rukun is a shared workspace for a family: spaces such as #sekolah and #rumah, each with a timeline of messages, tasks, lists and notes. It comes from the Ideation report for Digital Entrepreneurship Product Development, where it is a mobile and web app, and it draws on Slack, Linear and Obsidian. Tahap A builds this desktop slice of it with thor, Electron, Tauri and GPUI, and measures all four. Every app follows this page to the point. All sizes are in points. Each change after the first comparator app starts goes into the change list at the end, with its date.

1. The app

  • A sidebar with Tasks and the family's 12 spaces, and a page beside it.
  • Tasks: a searchable, virtualized list of 5,000 tasks. The app opens on this page.
  • A space: its timeline of messages, tasks, lists and notes, newest at the bottom. Items differ in height. #keluarga holds 4,000 items, 232 of them with a photo.
  • A note: its text, its checklist, its tasks, the notes it links to, and where it is mentioned. Read-only.
  • Writing: a message sent in a space, and a task added on the Tasks page. The app's user is Nadia (m1), who writes both. What she writes lasts until the app quits.
  • The Tasks page is also the Tahap B task. The Tahap B starter is this app with the Tasks page empty, and its task card leaves out the New task field.

4. Checkboxes and avatars

  • Task checkbox: a 16-point circle. Open: a 1.5-point ring in faint. Done: filled with accent, with a white checkmark 10 points wide.
  • List checkbox: a 14-point square with radius 4. Open: a 1.5-point border in faint. Done: filled with accent, with a white checkmark 9 points wide.
  • A click on a checkbox toggles it and does nothing else. A done item's text turns dim. Toggles last until the app quits. A task's checkbox shows the same state on every page.
  • Avatar: the member's image in a circle, 20 points in task rows and cards, 28 in a timeline.

5. Sidebar

  • Padding: 52 at the top (below the traffic lights), 12 at the sides and the bottom. It scrolls if the window is too short.
  • Rows are 32 high, full width, 2 apart, corner radius 6, label inset 12, Body strong. First Tasks, then a 1-point line in line colour with 8 of space above and below, then each space in data order as #name.
  • The row of the current page: background selected, text text. A note page counts as its note's space. Other rows: text dim, background hover under the pointer.
  • A click on Tasks shows the Tasks page. A click on a space shows its page, scrolled to the bottom.

The column headers and rows share one column rule: 16 5fr 2fr 2fr 88 with 16 between columns, inside the 12 of inset. In CSS this is grid-template-columns: 16px 5fr 2fr 2fr 88px; column-gap: 16px.

  • Order: data order, which is by due date, latest first.
  • Hover: a row under the pointer gets background hover.
  • Selection: a click on a row, away from its checkbox, selects that task and only it. The selected row gets #0a84ff2e, which wins over hover.
  • Search: each change shows the tasks whose title or assignee's name holds the query, ignoring case, in data order. The list goes back to its top. A hidden task stays selected.
  • Empty result: the list shows "No tasks match" in Body dim, centred in the list.
  • New task: Return adds a task when the field holds more than spaces. A word #name that names a space puts the task in that space and leaves the title. Otherwise the task goes in #keluarga. The task is Nadia's, open, and due today by the Mac's clock. It goes first in the list, ahead of the data order, and becomes the selected task, and the list goes back to its top. The search applies to it as to any task. The same task shows as a task card at the end of its space's timeline, by Nadia, at the time it was added. The field empties and keeps the focus.
  • The page keeps its query, its selection and the New task field's text while another page shows.
  • No scroll bar shows, here or on any page.

7. Space page

  • Padding 40 at the top and 24 at the sides and the bottom. A header row of height 36 with #name in Heading, then a gap of 8, then the timeline, which fills the rest and scrolls, with 16 of padding at its top and bottom, then a gap of 12 and the composer.
  • The composer: a text field the width of the page, 40 high, radius 8, inset 12, background hover. Body text, the placeholder "Message #name" in faint, and the search field's focus border.
  • Return sends what the composer holds when it holds more than spaces: a message by Nadia at the end of the timeline, timed by the Mac's clock. The composer empties and keeps the focus, and the timeline scrolls to its end. One draft serves every space, and it stays while another page shows.
  • Items run oldest at the top to newest at the bottom, 16 apart. The page opens scrolled to the bottom.
  • Each item: the author's avatar at 28, 12 of gap, then a column up to 640 wide. The column starts with a line of the author's name in Body strong text, 8 of gap, and the time as 8 Nov 2026, 21.26 in Small faint. Then 4 of gap and the content.
  • Message: the text in Body text, wrapping at the column's width. With a photo: 8 of gap, then the photo at 240 × 180, radius 8, cropped to fill.
  • Task: a card 360 × 40, radius 8, background card, a 1-point border in line, inset 12. The task checkbox, 10 of gap, the title in Body text, and the assignee's avatar at the right.
  • List: a card 360 wide, radius 8, background card, a 1-point border in line, padding 12. The title in Body strong text, 6 of gap, then one row of 24 per entry: the list checkbox, 8 of gap, the entry in Body text.
  • Note: a card 360 × 56, radius 8, background card, a 1-point border in line, inset 12. The note's title in Body strong text, then its first paragraph in Small dim on one line. A click opens the note page.
  • The timeline builds only the items on screen. Each framework measures item heights its own way.
  • A photo shows in place once it decodes, with no fade. Until then its place shows the empty colour.

8. Note page

  • The whole page scrolls as one, with padding 40 at the top, 24 at the sides and the bottom. Its content is 640 wide at most.
  • The note's title in Heading. Then 16 of gap and the paragraphs in Body text, wrapping, 12 apart.
  • Then each section that has entries, in this order, each after 24 of gap: a Subheading, 8 of gap, and its rows.
    • "Checklist": rows of 24, as in a list card.
    • "Tasks": rows of 34 with the task checkbox, the title and the due date, as on the Tasks page.
    • "Links": the notes it links to, by title.
    • "Mentioned in": first the spaces whose timeline holds the note, as #name, then the notes that link to it.
  • Link rows are 28 high, in Body accent. A click opens that note, or that space at the bottom.

9. Data

data/generate.py makes the data from a fixed seed. The output is committed, so every app reads the same bytes. It is synthetic: no person, chat or photo in it is real.

  • data/rukun.json, built into each app at compile time: 5 members, 12 spaces with 9,740 timeline items, 5,000 tasks over three years, and 200 notes. About 15% of messages end in an emoji, and about 10% of task titles are long enough to end in an ellipsis.
  • data/avatars/m1.jpg to m5.jpg: 56 × 56. data/photos/p0001.jpg to p0400.jpg: 480 × 360. Each is its largest size on a 2× screen. They ship as files in the .app.

10. Settings for the harness

Each app reads these environment variables and does nothing else for the tests. The harness reads the app's stdout.

VariableBehaviour
TESTAPP_MARK=1After the first frame that draws rows of the page it opened on, print the line first-frame and flush.
TESTAPP_PAGE=keluargaOpen on that space's page, scrolled to the bottom, in place of Tasks.
TESTAPP_SCROLL=1One second after the first frame, scroll the open page's list at 1,500 points per second for TESTAPP_SCROLL_SECONDS (20 by default): down on the Tasks page, up on a space. Turn back at either end. Set the scroll once per frame, in the display-driven callback, from the time since the scroll began. Record the monotonic time at the start of every frame. When the time is up, print one line per frame, frame <microseconds since the scroll began>, then done, and quit.

The display-driven callback is thor's display link, requestAnimationFrame in Electron and Tauri, and GPUI's frame callback. The web apps send their lines from the page to the main process, which prints them. For thor, backlog item 6 builds these into dev as THOR_STARTUP_MARK and THOR_BENCH, and the harness sets both names.

11. What each app does its own way

The report names each of these per app.

  • Text: the framework's own text stack and the system fonts. No web fonts.
  • Long lists: the framework's usual tool. TanStack Virtual for Electron and Tauri, with measured rows in the timeline. uniform_list for the Tasks page and list for the timeline in GPUI. kit.list in thor.
  • Images: the framework's usual way to show an image file. An <img> for the web apps, img() for GPUI, image for thor.
  • The checkmark: thor draws the SF Symbol checkmark. The other apps draw spec/icons/check.svg.
  • Hover may fade, for 0.35 s at most. Nothing else animates, so the app goes idle once the pointer stops.
  • Release builds use each framework's own release settings.

12. Out of scope

From the Ideation report: Today, the calendar, quick add, voice, asking AI, turning a message into a task, and editing notes. Also editing or deleting messages and tasks, setting a task's assignee or due date, sending photos, mentions, reactions, keyboard navigation beyond the search field, menus, drag and drop, light mode, saving anything between launches, and accessibility.
